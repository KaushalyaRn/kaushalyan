## Current work

Hoverflies are unusual pollinators: adults move pollen, while larvae hunt pests or recycle nutrients, depending on the species. Like other insects, they also carry microbial communities. I want to know who those microbes are, and whether they help Finnish hoverflies cope with environmental stress such as pesticides.

The questions in front of me now are:

- How much does **host species** shape the microbiome?
- What does **diet** do to those communities?
- Can microbes help flies cope with **pesticides** and other stressors?

The work so far is supported by the OLVI Foundation, with Dr. Anne Duplouy and Dr. Gunilla Ståhls at the University of Helsinki.
