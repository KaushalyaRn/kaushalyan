---
title: OLVI support for the hoverfly microbiome
date: 2026-04-01
summary: Personal grants from the OLVI Foundation are supporting the host, diet, and pesticide sides of the project.
---

The OLVI Foundation has supported this research with two personal grants:

- 2025 — host species and diet in microbial communities of Finnish hoverflies
- 2026 — effects of pesticide exposure on those communities

I am grateful for the space this gives to do the work carefully: collecting, sequencing, and asking what the microbes are doing for the fly, not only who is there.
