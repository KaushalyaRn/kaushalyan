title: Joining the hoverfly work
date: 2025-12-01
summary: I started as a grant-funded researcher in the ISEE lab at the University of Helsinki.
---

I joined the Insect Symbiosis Ecology and Evolution group in December 2025. The project is about Finnish hoverflies and the microbes they carry — how host species and diet shape those communities, and what happens when pesticides enter the picture.

The work sits in the Organismal and Evolutionary Biology Research Programme. Dr. Anne Duplouy and Dr. Gunilla Ståhls supervise.
